Contest Winners & Results

Categories

Each category gets its own 1st / 2nd / 3rd podium on the public Results page. Create any new category (e.g. Reel, Mobile Photography) and pick it for each winner below.

Winners

Add as many winners as you need. Choose a Category and a Position (1st / 2nd / 3rd) for each winner. In every category the top three appear as poster cards (swipe carousel on mobile), the rest under "More winners & mentions". Photos you upload here are saved to Cloudinary.

About Us page

Everything on the About Us page is edited here: change any text, add or remove items, reorder them with the arrows, or hide a whole block. Icons use Bootstrap Icons names, e.g. bi-camera-fill. Links can be #join, #events-head, https://... or mailto:.... Click Save About Us to publish.
